# Nitrite Assay 

Parsons, T. (2013). *A manual of chemical & biological methods for seawater analysis*. Elsevier

### Materials 
* 50ml Falcon tubes
* 15ml falcon tubes
* Cuvettes
* Spectrophotometer (@ 525 nm)
* Pipettes & Tips - 200, 1000ul 
* Kim Wipes
* Syringe filter
* 0.2um filters 
* 2x 250ml brown bottles 

### Chemicals 
* Hydrochloric acid
   * CAS 7647-01-0
   * MW 36.46g/mol
   * Store in acid cabinet
* Sulfilanilamide
   * CAS 63-74-1
   * MW 172.2g/mol
   * Store -------------
* N-(1-Naphthyl)ethylenediamine Dihydrochloride (NED)
   * CAS 1465-25-4
   * MW 259.17g/mol
   * Store --------------
* Sodium Nitrite
   * CAS 7632-00-0
   * MW 69g/mol
   * Store chemical shelf 

### Reagent Preparation 
1. 10% Hydrochloric acid
    1. Dilute concentrated HCl oceanography way
    2. Ex 250ml = 25ml HCl + 225ml MQ water 
3. Sulfilanilamide Solution
   1. In a brown bottle, combine 2.5g in 250ml 10% HCl 
   2. Store at room temperature 
4. NEDD Solution
   1. In a brown bottle, combine 0.25g in 250mL MQ
   2. Store at room temperature
5. Nitrite Working Stock (0.10M)
   1. Weigh ~ 0.345g of NaNO2 into a 50 mL Falcon tube
   2. Add up to 50 mL water, mix well
   3. Calculate exact concentration
      1. _______g/(69g/mol*50mL)*1,000,000 = __________mM
      2. Multiply by 1000 to get uM concentration
   4. Make Secondary (working) stock (1mM) from primary stock concentration
      1. (Primary stock concentration)___________uM x ______ml = 1000 µM x 50 mL
      2. ____________ uL = (1000uM*50ml/(primary stock concentration)__________uM)*1000
      3. Combine ___________ µL of primary working stock with up to 50ml MQ water
   5. Filter through a 0.2um filter to sterilize and store in fridge 

### Material Preparation 
1. Make standards
   1. Label 10x 50mL Falcon Tubes as follows:
      1. 10uM
      2. 5uM
      3. 2.5uM
      4. 1.25uM
      5. 0.625uM
      6. 0.3125uM
      7. 0.15625uM
      8. 0.078125uM
      9. 0.0390625uM
      10. 0uM
2. Rinse all tubes 3x with MQ water
3. Make 1000nM nitrate standard
   1. In the 1000nM tube, add 50 µL of 1mM NO2 working stock into 49.95mL MQ water
   2. Vortex
4. Serial Dilutions to make standards
   1. Add 25ml MQ water to each empty tube
   2. Add 25ml of the 1000nM standard into the 500nM standard tube
   3. Vortex
   4. Continue dilutions by taking 25ml from the previous concentration
   5. If continuing use, filter through a 0.2um filter to sterilize and store in fridge

### Primary Procedure
1. Aliquot 10ml of each sample/standard into a 15ml tube
2. Add 200ul of sulfanilamide to each tube 
   1. Incubate in the dark for 2-10 minutes
5. Add 200µL of NED to each tube
   1. Incubate 30mins-2hrs before quantifying
6. Turn on the spectrophotometer ~30min before measuring
7. Measure absorbance at 525nm
