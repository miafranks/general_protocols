### Cleaning Old (Acid) Bottles for Waste 
Description: How to clean/rinse an old HCl bottle to be used for nondescript waste

## Materials 
* Old HCl Bottle
* Citranox

## Safety 
* Work in the hood with all PPE (gloves, coat, glasses) 

## Procedure
1. Make sure HCl bottle is emptied of all possible acid.
2. In the hood, rinse the bottles 3x with DI water
   1. When adding the water, gently add it so it pours on the side of the bottle and does not splash into the bottom.
   2. Cap the lid and gently swirl the water all over the bottle 
4. Rinses may be disposed down the sink while running tap water (a lot)
5. If necessary, neutralize any remaining acid with a weak base such as sodium bicarbonate
   1. If you don’t have a weak base, you can skip this step and use a decent amount of soap and water after you do the initial 3 rinses.
7. Rinse with 2% citranox
8. Rinse with DI water 
9. Deface, cover, or scratch out the original HCl label on the container before repurposing it.
10. Make a new tag in RSS WASTe and place it on the bottle to use
